The problem02 / 09

You can recognise the answer on the page. Can you say it?

Who it is for

A student revising from their own text notes before an oral exam, a viva or a seminar, who has read the pages several times and never tried to explain them aloud.

Who it is not for

Scanned notes with no text layer, or anyone who wants their notes fact-checked. Aloud checks what you say against your notes, not the notes against the world.

Rereading and highlighting test recognition. Saying it with the page closed tests recall.

Alone, nobody asks the follow-up, and nothing pushes back when you nod along to a plausible sentence.

So Aloud pushes back. It states claims from your notes, half of them quietly altered, and the round is only won if you catch them.

The game04 / 09

Four kinds of level, one loop: play, score, come back.

Say it

Explain a concept. The examiner follows up. Code grades you against your pages.

Catch it

The examiner states a claim. Half are real, half altered. Call real or bluff.

Boss

A mixed round that ends each world, with 4 hearts.

Recall

Concepts you missed come back later as a level.

3 hearts a level. XP with a combo multiplier. Up to 3 stars. Ranks 1 to 30. A daily streak with freezes earned every 7 days. Rules are pure functions in src/lib/game, unit-tested.

Rules: docs/GAME-DESIGN.md. Tests: tests/game-scoring.test.ts, tests/game-progress.test.tsScreenshot, not a mockup
Catch it05 / 09

The examiner says something wrong. Do you notice?

The examiner says

Each token is not projected into three vectors: a query, a key, and a value.

Bluff: a negation was added

Your page, p. 7

Each token is projected into three vectors: a query (what this token is looking for), a key, and a value.

verify_claim(claim)
-> contradicted, page 7, quote checked as a verbatim substring of that page

What decides the round

The stored flag on the claim and your call. The page check supplies the proof card. No code reads the examiner's prose, so a model that misspeaks cannot change your score.

Where it falls short

On the sample run the page check proved 7 of 11 bluffs. It missed 3 and called 1 supported. Real claims: 8 of 8 proved. A miss costs the proof line, not points.

How AssemblyAI is used07 / 09

The examiner is the Voice Agent API. Everything it says about your notes goes through a tool.

PieceWhat Aloud does
Voice Agent APIBrowser WebSocket, 24 kHz PCM16 microphone audio up, examiner audio down.
Token flowA server route mints a token of at most 600 s per connection. The key is in no client file; a test scans for it.
Session configsession.update with the versioned prompt, three tools, key terms from your concepts, and a block for the level being played.
Native turn detectionOnly documented fields: vad_threshold, interrupt_response, interruption_delay. Undocumented names sent earlier were ignored by the service; probing found it.
Tool callingsearch_my_material, verify_claim, grade_my_answer, held until reply.done, answered with tool.result.
Barge-inOn input.speech.started playback is flushed and late audio is dropped.

Limits and links09 / 09

Where it stops, said plainly.

  • The game loop has not been played by voice with a human microphone in a browser. The take is the first run.
  • The claim judge is a model. Code proves the quote is on the page, not that the verdict is right. 54 labelled claims is a small set.
  • Typed Catch it rounds score from the flag and your tap. The correction field is not scored.
  • Barge-in stop time in a browser was not measured. Reconnect starts a new session with recent turns.
  • Chromium only. No phone, Firefox or Safari pass. Legal pages are drafts.
  • Without a database the store is a temporary file. Progress is also kept in the browser.
